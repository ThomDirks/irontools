What the ents leave you

More detail

Offerings are set by the totem's log type alone, not by what you decorate it with. Rummaging them is what yields research points and the drops: the Fletching knife at roughly 1 in 333 and the Greenman's mask at about 1 in 500, alongside ent branches and bow string spools. A hundred offerings is worth one research point at Ranulph, so the two currencies move together.
