More detail

Ordan's prices are fixed for every account type and never move. Stock caps at 100 of each ore; a Dwarven Miner tops up 20 to 40 of one random ore every 27 seconds, so a big order means hopping worlds or waiting between buyouts. Ordan stops at mithril, so adamantite and runite bars have to come from ore you mined yourself. Buying the finished adamant or rune item and melting it down avoids that entirely.

Is the foundry actually the best use of these bars?

More detail

Anvil smithing is a flat 12.5 experience per bar whatever you hammer out, so the foundry wins on experience for every alloy above the very bottom. Items are only worth making when you want the item itself, or when selling it back beats what Kovac pays.
