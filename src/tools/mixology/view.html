How it\'s worked out

Order xp is modelled on the wiki's order weights for every base you have unlocked, and the sim re-checks your level as xp comes in, so unlocking Mixalot mid-grind is accounted for. Multi-step potions (stamina, extended antifire, divine, sanfew) are not modelled. Goggles also save the toadflax in antidote+ (unf) and the irit in antidote++ (unf), which is not counted here.
