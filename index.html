How the numbers work

  • Each golem uses 1 fur and gives the "Crafting XP" shown. That number is the fur XP plus the flat 1,560 golem XP.
  • Each golem needs 5 sunstones (4 to shape it, 1 chiselled into a core). Mining XP = 5 × per-sunstone XP (23 normal, 28 with momentum). Chiselling each core also gives a small +10 Crafting XP that is not counted above.
  • Hunter XP assumes you catch every fur yourself. Buying furs from the Grand Exchange gives no Hunter XP. Wyrmscraig goat XP scales with your Hunter level (20 to lure with a cattleprod, plus 100 to 179 loot), so it is recalculated from your Hunter level.
  • Mining XP assumes you mine the sunstones yourself.

Sources

  • All fur, sunstone and Hunter values are from the OSRS Wiki (Golem crafting, Sunstone rocks, and each creature's Hunter page), current as of the Wyrmscraig release.

OrePrice eachStockRestockNotes

Ordan's prices are fixed for every account type and never move. Stock caps at 100 of each ore; a Dwarven Miner tops up 20 to 40 of one random ore every 27 seconds, so a big order means hopping worlds or waiting between buyouts. Ordan stops at mithril, so adamantite and runite bars have to come from ore you mined yourself. Buying the finished adamant or rune item and melting it down avoids that entirely.
